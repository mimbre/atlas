# Try UC-Atlas-24: Clarifications go to the producer

What you can do: pick up a scene that is too vague to design use cases from, and see that Design does not guess. Its agent asks its questions, and they go to the scene's producer as a To-Do. The producer answers them, or rewrites the scene, and Design carries on with a fresh pass of its agent. The same holds for a use case too vague to break down into issues: Plan asks the use case's producer, the person who supervised the Design run that made it (see [Plan asks the use case's producer](#plan-asks-the-use-cases-producer)). The run keeps its supervisor the whole time. Flow writes the questions, the producer writes the answers, and the history says so (SC-Atlas-09).

Every item records its producer (`produced_by`): Flow links each use case Design makes, and each issue Plan makes, to the person who supervised that run. A scene is put in from outside Atlas, so it records who put it in, in the same write that creates it: the person who creates it at the TUI, or, when an agent creates it (`atlas put`), the person that agent assists (see [A scene records who put it in](#a-scene-records-who-put-it-in)). Nobody chooses it, and nobody changes it afterwards. A use case or issue made by hand records none, and neither does an item from before producers were recorded. For such an item, its producer is the environment's configured decider (`actors.decider` in `products/software_factory/environments/test/core.json`). The To-Do goes to the producer even when someone else supervises the run, and the run's agent work still runs on the supervisor's Runtimes. So does **Accept** (Accept the scene), once all its use cases are accepted.

## Start

```sh
atlas tui --env test
```

You need a Runtime of yours connected to test, as for any pickup (`docs/guides/agent-cli.md`). `--env test` is needed: `atlas` on its own opens prod.

| The producer's To-Do | the producer's (yours, as you put the scene in) **Assigned to you**: the scene, `↵` | **What to do: Answer its questions**. **Why** says how it came to you, and **Its agent's questions** shows the questions. | Who wrote what | the To-Do's **Why** line, and each write's answer from Facts (in the TUI's `updated by hint` and in rela's history of the scene) | The questions were written by `flow`, cause `For <supervisor>: asked by TK-SC-Atlas-NN-1`, never by the agent. The answers, and any rewritten description, were written by the producer. Atlas keeps no history of its own (D-0100): ask the pinned rela (`docs/guides/rela.md`) how it shows a fact's changes. |

## A scene records who put it in

| Step | Who, where | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| A puts a scene in | A: TUI, `c` a scene, a title and a clear description, `ctrl-s` | The scene's page shows **Produced by  A**, set by A: `Produced by: A (since …, by A)` in `atlas get SC-Atlas-NN --env test`. A's create linked it in the same write. Nothing in the form asked for it. |

| An agent puts a scene in | `ATLAS_AS=coordinator atlas put scene --title "Newcomers" --description "…" --env test` | `atlas get` shows `Produced by: melle (since …, by coordinator: Put in by coordinator on behalf of melle)`. The write is the agent's, on behalf of its person, and its **Accept** goes to melle. |
| Setting it yourself is refused | `atlas put scene --title x --produced_by A --env test`, `atlas put scene --title x --link produced_by=A --env test`, or `atlas set SC-Atlas-NN produced_by=A --env test` | Refused every time. `atlas put` answers that the form does not ask for `produced_by`. ## What changed for this

- **The model DSL** (`products/software_factory/model/generator/lib/schema.ex`): a task that proposes (`creates:` or `writes:`) may also name `asks: [field:, file:]`. Such a task has one more result, `asked`, which its `on:` maps. The compile refuses `asks:` without a proposal, inside a ladder, or naming its proposal's file, and the description check refuses a field the subject lacks. An owner default may be `{kind, config: role, supervisor: false}`: the configured actor even inside a supervised span, for a state that asks someone other than the run's supervisor.
- **Flow** (`platform/atlas_flow/lib/atlas_flow/activities/task.ex`): the agent's instructions explain the questions file. When the task completed and its result has that file with any text in it, Flow, the only writer, puts the text in the subject's field, by `flow` and with the run's supervisor in its cause. It makes nothing of any proposal, records nothing to discard, and the task ends `asked`. If the file is missing or empty, nothing changes from before. `AtlasFlow.Model.owner/3` keeps a `supervisor: false` default with its configured actor.
- **The model** (`products/software_factory/model/contract/eyra/scene.ex`): the scene has `questions` (Flow's only, `set_by: [:flow]`) and `answers` (the producer's). Design's task asks to `questions.md` and reads `title`, `description`, `questions` and `answers`. Its new state `clarifying`, **Answer its questions**, lies in Design's span, so the run keeps its supervisor. It is owned by the configured decider, not the supervisor, shows the questions, and offers **Answered** with a note into `answers`, which goes back to `break_down`. There is no migration: since D-0102 a run continues in its state by name, and a new state needs none.
- **Control and the Console**: nothing new. The To-Do, its section and its answer come from the generated contract.
- **Tests**: `products/software_factory/model/contract/test/pipeline_runs_test.exs` and `sent_back_runs_test.exs` (the run end to end: asked, the producer's To-Do, Answered, a fresh pass with the questions, answers and current description, then use cases as before), `platform/atlas_flow/test/activities/proposal_test.exs` (questions set the field and make nothing; no file or an empty one, as before), `platform/atlas_flow/test/supervised_owner_test.exs`, and `products/software_factory/model/generator/test/schema_test.exs`.

For Plan, and for recording the producer:

- **The model DSL**: `producer relation: name` (`products/software_factory/model/generator/lib/description.ex`, checked in `description/build.ex`) names the relation from an item to its producer, written into the run mapping as `supervision.producer`. It needs supervision, is given once, and its relation must lead to one fact of the supervisor's kind with `set_by: [:flow]`. An owner default may be `{kind, config: role, producer: relation}` (`schema.ex`; `description/subflows.ex` checks that the relation leads from the subject to one fact of that kind): the subject's producer, and the configured actor when it links to nobody. It is never the run's supervisor.
- **Flow**: when a task's `creates:` proposal makes facts of a kind the producer relation leads from, each create also links them to the run's supervisor (`platform/atlas_flow/lib/atlas_flow/activities/task.ex`, the relation passed in from `runs/store.ex`). A run with no supervisor links nobody, and follow-ups a review makes are not linked. The projection passes the subject's producer link to `AtlasFlow.Model.owner/3` (`runs/projection.ex`), which resolves a producer owner as above.
- **The model**: the composition has `produced_by` (scene, use case or issue → person, at most one, `set_by: [:flow]`, so Facts refuses anyone but Flow) and `producer(relation: :produced_by)`. The use case (`products/software_factory/model/contract/eyra/use_case.ex`) has `questions` (Flow's only) and `answers`. Plan's task asks to `questions.md` and reads `title`, `description`, `design`, `questions` and `answers`. Its new state `clarifying`, **Answer its questions**, lies in Plan's span, is owned by the producer (`default: {:person, config: :decider, producer: :produced_by}`), shows the questions, and offers **Answered**, which goes back to `break_down`. Design's `clarifying` now asks the scene's producer the same way, with the same decider fallback, so it behaves as before for scenes made by hand. Neither needs a migration: since D-0102 a run continues in its state by name, and the DSL no longer has `migrate`.
- **Control and the Console**: nothing new. **Produced by** is the relation's label on an item's page, and the To-Do comes from the generated contract.
For a scene, who put it in (IS-Atlas-70):

- **rela first** (D-0100): the pinned rela 26.11.9 records no creator on an entity. `--run-as` only names `system:` and `integration:` identities in its audit. Its create triggers (`flows.yaml` `triggers:`, `{{current_user.id}}`) see rela's acting user, and that is always Facts. So rela cannot link a scene to the Atlas client who put it in, and the link is made with Facts' write rules and link origins, which Atlas already has.
- **The model**: the scene's `create` names `producer: :produced_by` (`products/software_factory/model/contract/eyra/scene.ex`), written into the extension as `entities.scene.create.producer`, the way `create.defaults` are. The description check (`generator/lib/description/check.ex`) refuses one that is no relation from the kind, leads to more than one fact, or is not `set_by`. **Accept** (`accept_scene` `to_accept`) is owned by `{:person, config: :decider, producer: :produced_by}`, as Design's `clarifying` is.
- **Facts** (`generator/lib/facts/writes.ex`): a relation marked `set_by` stays so, with one exception. The create of a kind whose `create.producer` names that relation may link it, in that write only, to whom the writer assists (`assists`, which Facts now passes with the writer). Otherwise it may link the writer itself, when the writer is a fact of the relation's kind. Any other key on that create is refused, and so is any change afterwards. No BEAM module names a kind.
- **Control** (`platform/atlas_control/lib/atlas_control/pages.ex` `put_in_by/4`, `session.ex` `create/5`): a create takes the link from who the client is: the person at the TUI, or whom the agent assists, with the cause `Put in by <agent> on behalf of <person>` (an agent's own cause is kept, with `(<agent> on behalf of <person>)` after it). A form or write that names `produced_by`, or any other `set_by` association, is refused before anything is sent. The Console needs nothing new: **Produced by** and its origin are on the page as for a use case.
- **Tests**: `products/software_factory/model/contract/test/scene_producer_test.exs`, beside `supervision_test.exs` and `sent_back_runs_test.exs`. It covers the extension; Facts taking the link from a person and from an assisting agent and refusing anyone else, any other key and any later change; Control's create in all three cases; the scene's page; and Accept going to the person, the agent's person, or the decider when none is recorded. Control's session end to end is in `platform/atlas_control/test/session_test.exs` (a member at the TUI, an assisting agent with and without its own cause, an agent that assists nobody, and a form that names it), and the check in `generator/test/description/checks_test.exs`.

For Plan, and the tests that record the producer:

- **Tests**: `products/software_factory/model/contract/test/sent_back_runs_test.exs` (Plan asks the producer: not its supervisor, not the decider; Answered resumes Plan with the questions and answers; issues then made as before, produced by Plan's supervisor; with no producer, the decider; and what Plan and Design propose links `produced_by` to their supervisor), `supervision_test.exs` (the relation, and Facts refusing anyone but Flow), `control_views_test.exs` (a use case's page shows **Produced by**), `platform/atlas_flow/test/activities/proposal_test.exs`, `platform/atlas_flow/test/supervised_owner_test.exs`, and `products/software_factory/model/generator/test/{supervision_test,schema_test}.exs`.
